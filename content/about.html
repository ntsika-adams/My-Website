Our History

TrendWear Clothing Store is a fictional South African fashion retailer established in 2026. The store provides affordable clothing, footwear and accessories for families across South Africa. TrendWear has built a loyal customer base through affordable prices, frequent promotions and a wide range of family-focused products.

Mission

To provide fashionable, affordable clothing for the whole family, backed by excellent and accessible customer service both in-store and online.

Vision

To become one of South Africa's leading value-fashion retailers by making shopping simple, convenient and accessible to every family, both in-store and online.

Our Target Customers TrendWear is aimed at:

- Budget-conscious families - Men and women looking for affordable everyday clothing

- Teenagers and young adults

- Parents shopping for children's clothing

- Customers looking for school uniforms and baby wear

- Customers who prefer online shopping
